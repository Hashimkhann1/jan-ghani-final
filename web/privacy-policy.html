Privacy Policy

App: Jan Ghani (com.janghani.janghani) · Last updated: 18 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Jan Ghani mobile app ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects information. The App is used by employees of Jan Ghani and by Jan Ghani's customers to view business reports and their own credit and installment details.

1. Who uses the App

  • Employees (accountants and authorised staff): sign in with an account and can view business and branch reports.
  • Customers: can view their own credit balance and installment schedule and payment status.

Users can create an account (sign up) with an email address and password, log in with those credentials, or choose "Sign in with Google".

The App is a viewing tool. Installments, credit entries and payments are recorded and managed by the Jan Ghani office installment system, not by the App. Customers cannot change their account data through the App.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, username or email address, role, and login credentials (passwords are handled by our authentication service and are not stored in readable form).
  • Google Sign-In information: if you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your Google account name, email address and profile picture from Google. We do not receive your Google password and we do not access your Gmail, contacts, Drive or other Google data.
  • Customer information: name, phone number, address, ID or account number, and photo where the company has recorded it.
  • Financial records: credit balances, invoices, installment plans, due dates and payment history.
  • Business records shown to employees: sales, branch and employee reports.
  • Technical data: basic app and device information (such as app version and error logs) needed to keep the App working.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, SMS, microphone, camera recordings or advertising identifiers. The App only needs internet access.

3. How we use information

  • To authenticate users and control who can see which data.
  • To show employees their reports and show customers their own credit and installment information.
  • To maintain, secure and improve the App and to investigate problems or misuse.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Sharing of information

Information is not shared with third parties except:

  • Service providers that host our data and authentication (our cloud database and authentication provider, Supabase) and Google (only if you use Google Sign-In, under Google's Privacy Policy) that process data only to provide their service.
  • Legal requirements, where disclosure is required by law or to protect rights and safety.

Each customer can only see their own records. Employees can only see the data allowed by their role.

5. Data security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and access is restricted by account and role. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

6. Data retention

We keep records for as long as needed for business, accounting and legal purposes, or while your account is active. Financial records may need to be kept after an account is closed.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to correct your information or to delete your account and related personal data (subject to legal and accounting record-keeping duties). To make a request, contact us using the details below. Customers can also contact the Jan Ghani office directly.

8. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the "Last updated" date changed.
